# Third-Party Fonts

inscanvas embeds local font files so the app and public pages do not depend on external font CDNs.

## Noto Serif SC
- Purpose: premium Songti-style display typography for public pages, titles, and selected headings.
- Source: Google Noto / Source Han Serif family.
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- Local notice: `public/fonts/OFL-Noto-Serif-SC.txt`.

## Fusion Pixel 10px Monospaced SC
- Purpose: short playful title accents, brand marks, and compact pixel labels.
- Source: TakWolf Fusion Pixel Font.
- License: SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- Local notice: `public/fonts/OFL-Fusion-Pixel.txt`.

Dense application controls should keep the HarmonyOS/system sans stack for readability. Pixel fonts are intentionally limited to short text.
